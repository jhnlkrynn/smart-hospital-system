# Laboratory Result Security

Laboratory security rules:

- Request and accession numbers are generated server-side.
- Patient and doctor identity are derived from trusted records.
- Draft, unverified, and unreleased results are hidden from patients.
- Technical and internal notes are hidden from patients.
- Attachments are stored privately and downloaded through authorized routes.
- Result release and amendment are audited.
- No AI interpretation, prescriptions, billing, or pharmacy records are created.
