# Patient Management

Phase 5 implements patient registration and identity management without adding appointment, queue, consultation, billing, or laboratory workflows.

## Implemented Scope

- Staff-assisted patient registration from `/admin/patients/create`.
- Patient accounts linked one-to-one through `patients.user_id`.
- Patient profiles without login accounts for walk-in or administrative registration.
- Searchable patient index with status, registration date, and basic identity filters.
- Profile photo upload to the public disk.
- Soft archive and restore for patient profiles.
- Emergency contacts, allergies, conditions, and private documents as separate child records.
- Audit logs for creation, update, archive, restore, QR regeneration, QR lookup, document upload, document download, and staff views.

## Access Rules

Super Admin and Hospital Admin can manage patient records. Nurses can create and update patient demographics for registration support. Doctors, pharmacists, laboratory staff, and cashiers only receive the patient permissions needed by their current or future workflows.

Cashier-facing patient views intentionally expose basic identity only. Clinical sections such as allergies, conditions, emergency contacts, and documents are hidden unless the signed-in user has the matching patient management permission.

## Patient Number

Patient numbers are generated by `App\Services\ReferenceNumberService` using the `PAT` prefix:

```text
PAT-YYYY-000001
```

The year uses the Asia/Manila timezone. Generation uses the locked `reference_sequences` table and a unique database constraint.

## Duplicate Handling

The create form checks for possible duplicates by normalized name, date of birth, contact number, or email. Possible duplicates are displayed as warnings, but they do not automatically block registration.

## Deliberately Not Included

Phase 5 does not include appointment booking, doctor assignment, queueing, triage, vital signs, consultations, prescriptions, lab requests, billing, payments, SMS, or AI diagnosis.
