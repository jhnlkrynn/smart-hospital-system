# Appointment Management

Phase 6 implements appointment scheduling without queue, triage, consultation, laboratory, pharmacy, billing, or payment workflows.

## Implemented Scope

- Staff-assisted appointment booking from `/admin/appointments/create`.
- Patient self-service booking from `/patient/appointments/create`.
- Doctor assigned appointment views from `/doctor/appointments`.
- Appointment search, status filters, date filters, sorting, and pagination.
- Appointment approval, rejection, cancellation, rescheduling, completion, and no-show marking.
- Appointment status history for each transition.
- Live dashboard appointment metrics.
- Audit logging for booking and status transitions.

## Appointment Identity

Appointment numbers are generated by `App\Services\ReferenceNumberService`:

```text
APT-YYYY-000001
```

The year uses the Asia/Manila hospital timezone. The sequence uses the locked `reference_sequences` table and the `appointments.appointment_number` unique constraint.

## Booking Sources

Appointments use `App\Enums\AppointmentSource`:

- `patient_portal`
- `staff`
- `admin`
- `doctor`
- `walk_in`

The `walk_in` source is reserved for a future queue/check-in phase.

## Staff Booking

Staff booking resolves the patient, doctor employee, department, appointment type, date, and slot on the server. The selected doctor must be an active doctor employee. The department is copied from the doctor's employee profile; it is not trusted from browser input.

## Patient Booking

Patient booking ignores any submitted `patient_id`. The patient is resolved from the authenticated user's linked patient profile. Patients cannot submit appointment numbers or protected statuses.

## Rescheduling

Rescheduling preserves history by creating a new appointment linked through `parent_appointment_id`, then marking the original appointment as `rescheduled`. The old appointment no longer blocks future slot availability.
