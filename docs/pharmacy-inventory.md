# Pharmacy Inventory

Inventory is tracked by medication stock batch and pharmacy location. Each batch records lot number, expiration date, on-hand quantity, reserved quantity, unit cost, and operational status.

Phase 10 supports purchase receiving, stock adjustments, quarantine/unquarantine, FEFO reservation, reservation release, and inventory ledger entries.

Dispensing, billing, payment posting, and medicine handover are reserved for Phase 11.
