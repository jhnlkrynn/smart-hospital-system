# QR Patient Identification

Phase 5 adds QR-based patient lookup for authorized staff.

## Token Design

Each patient receives a unique random QR token generated by `App\Services\Patient\PatientQrService`.

The token is stored separately from patient identity fields and is hidden from model array and JSON output. QR images encode a temporary signed route URL, not raw patient data.

## Lookup Flow

Authorized staff use `/patient-lookup`.

1. Scan or enter the QR token.
2. The system resolves the token to a patient profile.
3. The lookup result shows minimal identity and role-permitted clinical context.
4. The lookup attempt is audited.

The lookup route requires authentication, a verified active account, `patients.lookup-qr`, and request throttling.

## Regeneration

Authorized patient managers can regenerate a patient's QR token from the patient detail page. Regeneration invalidates the previous token immediately. Old-token lookup attempts return a not-found response and are audited without exposing the token.

## Patient QR Card

Patients can view their own QR card at `/patient/qr-card`. The card displays the patient number, name, and QR image only.
