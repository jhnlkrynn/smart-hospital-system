# Medication Catalog

Phase 10 adds a formulary-oriented medication catalog for pharmacy and prescribing workflows.

Records include generic name, optional brand name, category, dosage form, strength, manufacturer, default route/frequency, formulary status, high-alert flags, controlled medication flags, cold-storage flags, and archive state.

Inactive, discontinued, and out-of-formulary medications are blocked by the prescription service. Restricted medications require formulary management permission.
